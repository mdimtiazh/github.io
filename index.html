I am an AI researcher and educator at Kyung Hee University, South Korea, where I hold dual roles as Research Professor in the Dept. of CSE and Lecturer in the Dept. of Artificial Intelligence. My research centers on knowledge distillation, representation learning, and efficient deep learning — building compact models that perform like large ones by carefully transferring and preserving knowledge.

I teach graduate-level courses including Convolutional Neural Networks and AI Creative Research Projects to Master's and PhD students. My broader research tackles real-world challenges such as 3D pose estimation under occlusion, catastrophic forgetting in continual learning, multimodal action understanding, and deploying AI on low-resource edge devices. My work has been published in top-tier venues including ICLR (A*), IEEE TCSVT, and Elsevier IP&M.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

🧠

Knowledge Distillation

Novel training strategies for transferring representational expertise from teacher to student models, enhancing lightweight architectures without sacrificing performance.

👁️

Computer Vision

3D human pose estimation, occlusion-aware learning, moving object detection, and multi-person scene understanding in real-world scenarios.

⚡

Efficient Deep Learning

Designing lightweight, computation-aware architectures for deployment on edge and resource-constrained devices without compromising accuracy.

🔄

Continual Learning

Tackling catastrophic forgetting and enabling models to adapt to new tasks while preserving previously acquired knowledge and representations.

🌐

Multimodal Learning

Robust learning frameworks that integrate and process diverse data modalities including vision, action, and language for real-world AI applications.

🤖

Foundation Models & LLMs

Developing lightweight LLM foundational models optimized for low-resource devices and edge computing environments.
